# **STEP 5 · REBUTTAL / PIVOT**

*COACH: Only if they hesitate. Your best rebuttal is their own Recap. Full rebuttal SCs are on the rebuttal sheet.*

### **The AIOA Framework**

*AIOA gives every LO a simple repeatable structure. It keeps the call calm, curious, and controlled instead of sounding like a canned rebuttal.*

| Step | Meaning | Purpose | Example Line |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | **Agree & Acknowledge** | Validate the concern first. Lower resistance before solving. | "I completely understand why you'd feel that way." |
| **I** | **Isolate** | Find the real issue underneath the first objection. | "Besides the payment, is there anything else holding you back?" |
| **O** | **Overcome** | Answer the actual concern with clarity, education, options, or a reframe. | "Here's why this still helps your overall financial picture..." |
| **A** | **Ask** | Confirm resolution and move the conversation forward. | "Knowing that, are you comfortable moving forward?" |
